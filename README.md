# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes customer purchasing behavior using **Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI**.

The project uses transactional data containing **3,900 customer purchases across different product categories** to identify spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discount behavior, subscription trends, and revenue contributions.

The objective is to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven marketing, customer engagement, loyalty, and product strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve:

- Sales performance
- Customer engagement
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer loyalty
- Marketing strategies
- Product positioning

The project investigates purchasing patterns across customer demographics, product categories, discounts, reviews, seasons, subscriptions, and shipping preferences.

### Main Business Question

> How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 purchase records and 18 columns**.

### Key Features

- Customer demographics
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Location
  - Subscription Status

- Purchase information
  - Item Purchased
  - Category
  - Purchase Amount
  - Season
  - Size
  - Color

- Shopping behavior
  - Discount Applied
  - Promo Code Used
  - Previous Purchases
  - Frequency of Purchases
  - Review Rating
  - Shipping Type

### Data Quality

- Total Records: **3,900**
- Total Columns: **18**
- Missing Review Ratings: **37**
